# 10-deal EDGAR pilot

## What matters

- **10** technology acquisitions reviewed; **114** acquirer-side filings and **1,329** documents retrieved. *(8-Ks, S-4s, 424B3 prospectuses, tender-offer filings, and exhibits.)*
- **8 of 10** had an acquirer-side agreement exhibit. *(Usually EX-2.1: merger or purchase agreement.)*
- **8 of 10** had an employee-related disclosure. *(8-K announcements or EX-2.1 agreement exhibits.)*
- **1 of 10** disclosed a specific retention/transaction package: **Intuit–Mailchimp**. *(8-K announcement and EX-2.1.)*

## Overall findings

- **Fully public:** Intuit–Mailchimp disclosed a retention pool, transaction bonuses, and post-closing RSUs.
- **Partly public:** seven other deals disclosed something about people—continued employment, equity treatment, key-employee conditions, retention-bonus liabilities, or employee schedules—but not a complete retention package or total cost.
- **Not observed:** Fastly–Glitch and Take-Two–Zynga had no employee term in the first-pass review. Microsoft–Nuance had a public leadership-continuity statement but no agreement exhibit on the acquirer-side route.
- **Still private or unobservable:** side employment agreements, unfiled schedules, person-level pay, and whether a retention term actually kept someone at the firm.

## Why this pilot mattered

- It showed that public filings can support a deal-level dataset of employee-related terms.
- It showed the dataset must separate **disclosed**, **not disclosed**, **not retrieved**, and **not observable**. A blank cannot mean “no retention.”
- It narrowed the next step: expand the reviewed sample and code disclosure types before trying to measure employee outcomes or automate everything.

## Deals and filings

- **[Intuit–Mailchimp](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/896878/000119312521271682/d226456dex21.htm)** — *8-K*
  - Employment Matters section, retention pool, and transaction bonuses.
  - Announcement also disclosed assumed employee bonuses and post-closing RSUs.

- **[Clarivate–ProQuest](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1764046/000110465921067259/tm2116608d1_ex2-1.htm)** — *8-K, 424B3*
  - Covered continuing-employee pay, benefits, and bonus plans.
  - Minimum-benefit protection explicitly excluded retention, transition, and stay arrangements.

- **[Oracle–Cerner](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1341439/000119312521363742/d235675dex21.htm)** — *8-K, SC TO-T, SC TO-T/A*
  - Covered company equity awards and continued-service vesting.
  - No specific retention package found in first-pass review.

- **[Fastly–Glitch](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1517413/000151741322000075/fastly-pressreleasenoterep.htm)** — *8-K*
  - Acquisition announcement found; no acquirer-side Exhibit 2 agreement retrieved.
  - No employee term found.

- **[Microsoft–Nuance](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/789019/000119312521084239/d130687dex991.htm)** — *8-K*
  - Nuance CEO Mark Benjamin was to remain CEO and report to Microsoft Cloud and AI leadership.
  - No acquirer-side agreement exhibit or retention package found.

- **[Okta–Auth0](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1660134/000119312521156436/d145794dex21.htm)** — *8-K*
  - Founders and key employees had to remain employed immediately before closing.
  - No public retention-payment amount found.

- **[Roper–Frontline](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/882835/000119312522233743/d356123dex21.htm)** — *8-K*
  - Outstanding retention bonuses were treated as a liability.
  - No total amount was disclosed.

- **[Take-Two–Zynga](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/946581/000119312522005771/d282059dex21.htm)** — *8-K, S-4, S-4/A, 424B3, SC TO-I, SC TO-I/A*
  - Merger agreement and award-treatment terms found.
  - No specific retention package found in first-pass review.

- **[Unity–ironSource](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1810806/000119312522193960/d292378dex21.htm)** — *8-K, S-4, S-4/A, 424B3*
  - Required reasonable best efforts to keep key employees available before closing.
  - No public package amount found.

- **[Skyworks–Silicon Labs Analog unit](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/4127/000110465921053805/tm2113063d1_ex2-1.htm)** — *8-K*
  - Employee-matters provisions and employee/key-employee schedules were present.
  - The schedules themselves were not public.

## Limit

- “Not found” means not found in this first-pass, acquirer-side review. It does not mean the term did not exist.
